Five-season survey across Ghana's Coastal Savannah (Kpong) and Deciduous Forest
(Aseseeso) agroecological zones, quantifying how weather variables, host-plant
phenology, and natural enemies jointly shape cabbage aphid (*Brevicoryne brassicae*
and *Lipaphis pseudobrassicae*) population dynamics.
