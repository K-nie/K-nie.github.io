I am a PhD Candidate in the [Genetics Graduate Program](https://genetics.wisc.edu)
at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, working in the
[Hittinger Lab](https://hittinger.genetics.wisc.edu) on yeast comparative and
evolutionary genomics.

<ul class="interest-tags">
  <li>Comparative genomics</li>
  <li>Machine learning for genomics</li>
  <li>Phylogenomics</li>
  <li>Yeast functional genetics</li>
  <li>Quantitative genetics</li>
</ul>

My research uses the **Y1000+** dataset, a panel of 1,154 species spanning the
Saccharomycotina subphylum, to ask how gene gain, loss, and modification shape
carbon-source metabolism, and how machine learning can connect genome content to
growth phenotypes. I am particularly interested in mannose biology and the hexose
transporter family, and in how comparative genomics can be used to predict
function in non-model fungi.

## Current focus

- **Y1000+ machine learning.** Classification models linking orthogroup content
  to carbon-source growth phenotypes across 1,154 Saccharomycotina species.
- **Phylogenomics.** Selection tests, ancestral state reconstruction, and gene-tree
  to species-tree reconciliation in the yeast subphylum.
- **HXT and PMI40 functional genetics.** CRISPR-Cas9 mutagenesis, growth-curve
  phenotyping, and uptake assays for hexose transport and mannose metabolism.
