Research interests

My research investigates gene regulation, metabolic adaptation, and evolutionary processes in yeast. I combine genetics, comparative genomics, and machine learning on large-scale phylogenomic datasets to study how yeast strains gain and lose the capacity to metabolize alternative carbon sources. I focus on uncovering the regulatory and evolutionary basis of sugar metabolism — particularly mannose and the hexose-transporter family — and on building open-source software that helps researchers identify yeast strains with unusual metabolic capabilities and the genes that drive them. The work sits at the intersection of comparative genomics, predictive modelling, and empirical genetics, with applications in metabolic engineering, synthetic biology, and industrial fermentation.
